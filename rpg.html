ART RPG

Auralym has an optional, custom RPG system where you can participate in events using stats and your art and prompt participation. Level up your stats with items and use them to boost points in community events such as boss fights and warring factions. This system is a living one and will receive updates over time.

Stats

a

MIGHT

Muscle and hard-hitting attacks. This stat corresponds to the number of dice rolled for Strength-based attacks.

FINESSE

Speed and flexibility. This stat corresponds to the number of dice rolled for Dexterity-based attacks.

WIT

Smarts and hitting where it counts. This stat corresponds to the number of dice rolled for Smarts-based attacks.

VIGOR

Vitality and endurance. This stat is a representation of your Auralym’s stamina and health, and is multiplied by 5 to get your stamina pool. If Vigor is 0, your stamina is 1.

AURA

Pure, unadulterated willpower. This stat corresponds to the type of dice rolled for attacks, starting at a d4 and maxing out at a d12.

Increasing Stats

Stats are limited by two factors: the overall stat points in total used and the individual stat cap. The overall stat point limit is 14 points across all stats (excluding Aura, as it starts at value 1). These can be distributed however you’d like. Each stat has a max value of 5 points. Aura’s sliding scale naturally limits it to 5 points. These limits may go up in the future if it is determined to be fitting to do so.

Stats are commonly increased by items, purchasable in the shop or receivable as rewards in events.

  • Militant Mace, used to upgrade Might
  • Fickle Feather, used to upgrade Finesse
  • Wise Whisker, used to upgrade Wit
  • Viscous Vial, used to upgrade Vigor
  • Auspicious Autograph, used to upgrade Aura
You may reset your stat array using the Charcoal Cleanser item. This item excludes Aura for fairness, but lets you reallocate existing points across the other 4 stats.

Stat Uses

Stamina

Your stamina represents the amount of times you can enter a combat as a specific character. Each combat has its natural ‘damage’ value to this stat, which reduces your stamina to a minimum of 0. If you have less stamina than the damage value for the combat, you can still enter that character until their stamina is 0. This value naturally regenerates every month as well. This feature is mostly meant to encourage using multiple characters during boss fights.
That being said, the Stamina Salve, found in the shop, restores your character’s stamina back to full.

Attacks

Each attack uses the quantity of one of the three damage stats (Might, Finesse, or Wit) combined with the die size from the Aura stat. As an example, if you do a Wit attack and have a Wit value of 2 and an Aura value of d6, the attack would end up rolling 2d6.

Your base damage per attack is counted as the number of corresponding dice from related stats + the amount of souls the attack garners / 25. Damage will be rolled by the moderator staff after an attack is approved. Continuing our example of a Wit-attack with 2d6, if we submitted a prompt that earned 250 souls, the damage roll would be 2d6+10.
If your respective stat has 0 points invested in it, you roll no dice and only gain the damage the attack garners from souls rewarded from the prompt.
