ART RPG

Auralym has an optional, custom RPG system where you can participate in events using a character's stats and your art and prompt participation. Level up your character's stats with items and use them to deal damage in community events such as boss fights and warring factions. This system is a living one and will receive updates over time, as well as adjustments as deemed necessary.

Stats

The system relies primarily on 5 stats - 3 meant for attacks, 1 for calculating stamina, and 1 for overall willpower of your Auralym. These are intrinsically tied to each character and must be leveled up seperately, hopefully allowing a variety of builds that fit the nature of your Auralym.

MIGHT

Muscle and hard-hitting attacks. This stat corresponds to the number of dice rolled for Strength-based attacks.

FINESSE

Speed and flexibility. This stat corresponds to the number of dice rolled for Dexterity-based attacks.

WIT

Smarts and hitting where it counts. This stat corresponds to the number of dice rolled for Smarts-based attacks.

VIGOR

Vitality and endurance. This stat is a representation of your Auralym’s stamina and health, and is multiplied by 5 to get your stamina pool. If Vigor is 0, your stamina is 1.

AURA

Pure, unadulterated willpower. This stat corresponds to a bonus applied to ALL attacks, regardless of their damage stat.

Increasing Stats

Stats are limited by two factors: the overall stat points in total used and the individual stat cap.
The overall stat point limit is 14 points across all stats excluding Aura. These can be distributed however you’d like using items, and naturally start at a value of 1.
Each stat has a max individual value of 5 points.
These limits may go up in the future if it is determined to be fitting to do so.

Stats are commonly increased by items, purchasable in the shop or receivable as rewards in events.

  • Militant Mace, used to upgrade Might
  • Fickle Feather, used to upgrade Finesse
  • Wise Whisker, used to upgrade Wit
  • Viscous Vial, used to upgrade Vigor
  • Auspicious Autograph, used to upgrade Aura
You may reset your stat array using the Charcoal Cleanser item. This item excludes Aura for fairness, but lets you reallocate existing points across the other 4 stats. This is also the only way to have a stat set to 0, if you desire.

Stat Uses

Stamina

Your stamina represents the amount of times you can enter a combat as a specific Auralym. Each combat has its natural ‘damage’ value to this stat, which reduces your stamina to a minimum of 0. If you have less stamina than the damage value for the combat, you can still enter that Auralym until their stamina is 0. This value naturally regenerates every month as well. This feature is mostly meant to encourage using multiple characters during boss fights.
That being said, the Stamina Salve, found in the shop, restores your character’s stamina back to full.

Attacks

At current, attacks are considered successful once content has been submitted meeting the combat's requirements and approved by a moderator. There is no current value threshold for 'success', nor a system in place for direct conflict between two Auralyms. This may be added in the future if desired.

Each attack uses Might, Finesse, or Wit to determine the number of d6 that are rolled. As an example, if you do a Wit attack and have a Wit value of 2, the attack would end up rolling 2d6.

Your base damage per attack is counted as the rolled total + your Aura stat + (the amount of souls the attack garners / 25). Only the prompt rewards are divided by 25, not the whole equation. Damage may be rolled in the appropriate channel when an attack is submitted and be linked into the submission. Continuing our example of a Wit-attack with 2d6, if we submitted a prompt that earned 250 souls, and we had an Aura stat of 1, the damage roll would be 2d6+1+10.
If your respective stat has 0 points invested in it, you roll no dice and only gain the damage the attack garners from souls rewarded from the prompt and your Aura stat. In the case of the example, it would end up being 0+1+10 for calculating damage.

Damage itself will be calculated by members as well as the prompt soul total and be verified by Staff upon approval.

Combat

Combat must be done with either your own Auralym or the Auralym of the Season. Other characters may be included in art to increase the souls earned, but only the focus character’s stats are counted for the attack’s initial damage as determined by the prompt. In the case where your own character and an Auralym of the Season are in the same piece, the main focus character will be whose stats are used. If both are equally important, it is up to the artist whose stats to use.
There are two types of combats: Boss Fights and Skirmishes.

Boss Fights are mod-hosted events that involve all players joining together to reach a common goal. This may include an actual boss to fight, or another common goal that may see Lilim and Aurim “working together”, for once. Boss fights typically have stretch goals that can be earned by hitting certain damage thresholds throughout the fight, and may be weak to certain kinds of damage. They may also come with gimmicks or their own personal requirements, although not always the case.

Skirmishes are mod-hosted events that divide the players into teams fighting against each other. It may be straight Aurim vs Lilim, or be squabbles between two or more factions. This feature will be coming out in the future with more details.

Auralym of the Season

The Auralyms of the Season are able to be used by anyone in combat when it arises. They also gain the following bonuses during their term:

  • Infinite Stamina
  • A 25% damage boost to any attacks they feature in
  • 50 Souls per submitted content they are included in.
